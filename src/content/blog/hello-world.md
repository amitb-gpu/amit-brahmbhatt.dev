Welcome to my corner of the internet.

I'm Amit — a software engineering leader and technical founder working across AI systems, cybersecurity, and high-performance computing. This site is three things:

1. **A blog.** Notes on what I'm learning and building — AI infrastructure, GPU computing, security, and the occasional detour into markets and options trading.
2. **A project showcase.** As new repos ship, they land on the [projects page](/projects) with a short write-up of what they do and why they exist.
3. **An idea garden.** Half-formed thoughts live on the [ideas page](/ideas). Some will become projects; most will just sit there, and that's fine.

Everything here is written by me, in my own words. If something's wrong or you'd build it differently, I'd genuinely like to hear about it — find me on LinkedIn.

New posts whenever there's something worth writing down.
